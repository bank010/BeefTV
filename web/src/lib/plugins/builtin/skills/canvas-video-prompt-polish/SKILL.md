# Canvas Video Prompt Polish

Keep the user's subject, action, reference intent, duration, and camera direction. Write a continuous shot with a clear start, motion, and end state. Distinguish subject motion from camera motion and maintain identity, scene, and lighting continuity. Do not invent extra shots, dialogue, or unsupported model controls.
